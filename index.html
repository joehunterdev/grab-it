Start Recording

💡 Tip: Your recording is saved locally in your browser. No data is sent to servers.

How to Use

1

Click Start Recording

Press the button to begin. Your browser will ask which screen or window to capture.

2

Choose Your Display

Select your screen, window, or browser tab from the system dialog that appears.

3

Audio Settings

When prompted, choose whether to include system audio in your recording.

4

Stop Recording

Click the red Stop button to finish. Your video appears above instantly.

5

Download Your Video

Right-click the video player and select "Save video as" to download your MP4 file.
